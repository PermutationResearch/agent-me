# Third-party UI components

Agent-Me adapts these components by Zepa UI:

- [Beam Zepa](https://zepa.design/components/beam-zepa), from the public [component registry](https://zepa.design/r/beam-zepa.json), retrieved October 25, 2026. Used in `src/ui/BeamZepa.tsx` and styled for the activity flow.
- [Timeline Zepa](https://zepa.design/components/timeline-zepa), from the public [component registry](https://zepa.design/r/timeline-zepa.json), retrieved October 25, 2026. Used in `src/ui/TimelineZepa.tsx` and adapted to actual submission status.

Both upstream components include this usage notice, preserved in their source:

> Free to use in your projects. Please don't republish or resell as your own.

These components remain attributed to Zepa UI. Their source headers and usage
terms govern those files; they are not represented as original Agent-Me work.

Geist Sans is distributed under the SIL Open Font License. The font and its
license are included in `public/fonts/Geist-Variable.woff2` and
`public/fonts/Geist-OFL.txt`.

## Instrument fonts

Instrument Sans and Instrument Serif are distributed under the SIL Open Font
License. The variable Sans and italic Serif files are served locally from
`public/fonts/Instrument-Sans.ttf` and
`public/fonts/Instrument-Serif-Italic.ttf`; their notices are
`Instrument-Sans-OFL.txt` and `Instrument-Serif-OFL.txt` in the same folder.
Source: the official [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts),
folders `ofl/instrumentsans` and `ofl/instrumentserif`.

The Clonk reference site was inspected to reconstruct its design patterns.
Agent-Me uses original implementation and generated artwork; Clonk's code,
artwork, logos, testimonials, prices, and commercial claims are not included.
