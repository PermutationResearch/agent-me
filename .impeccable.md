## Design Context

### Users

Agent-Me is for an individual owner operating a private AI twin on their own machine. Their primary jobs are to unlock the private workspace, review and curate proposed memories, ask questions grounded in those memories, and inspect how more advanced workflows reached an answer. The interface should work as a daily personal intelligence console, not as a developer demo.

### Brand Personality

Precise, trustworthy, composed. The product should carry the operational confidence and finish associated with Cloudflare and Vercel: direct language, excellent hierarchy, restrained visual treatment, and visible system state. Privacy and owner control should feel foundational rather than defensive.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light-first infrastructure console with warm monochrome surfaces, crisp hairline borders, compact navigation, and a sparing signal-orange accent. Use strong typographic hierarchy, small status indicators, and technical metadata over a clean, pattern-free canvas. Avoid background grids, neon AI gradients, glassmorphism, oversized marketing heroes, excessive rounded cards, and ornamental chatbot imagery.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with the owner’s next action: unlock, review memory, or ask the twin.
2. Make local/private/provider state visible at a glance and understandable in plain language.
3. Reveal workflow sophistication progressively; Standard is direct, Multi-agent is inspectable, and Verified adds a final gate.
4. Use borders, spacing, and typography for hierarchy before adding containers or decoration.
5. Keep every control accessible, keyboard-visible, responsive, and comfortable at 200% zoom.
